All data in `fixtures/`, `evaluation/cases/`, `evaluation/labels/` and the authored freeze manifest is authored for this repository and distributed under the MIT license in `LICENSE`. Entity/vendor names, transactions, invoice identifiers and amounts are fictional. No bank account, personal record, client data or third-party dataset is included.

The expected outcomes define a narrow engineering fixture. They are not an external accounting benchmark or professional accounting policy.

Twelve development bundles and 32 held-out bundles are synthetic. The 155 held-out input/label hashes were frozen before expanding the scripted policy. Hash consistency does not prove label accuracy or representative coverage. The fixture authoring tool refuses to overwrite an existing freeze; contributors must preserve these inputs and labels.
